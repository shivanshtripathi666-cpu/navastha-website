Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains how the Navastha website handles information. Navastha is a student technology platform that is still being built. This website is informational only.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through this website. There are no accounts, forms, sign-ups, payments or analytics tools, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Information stored on your device

The website saves your light or dark theme choice in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can clear it any time in your browser settings.

Third-party services

  • Fonts: The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. When a page loads, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Hosting: The website is hosted on a third-party static hosting service, which may keep standard server logs (for example IP address and time of visit) for security and operation.

Children

This website does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

Navastha is in development. When we launch apps, accounts or other features that handle personal information, we will update this policy before they go live and change the date above.
